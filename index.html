About

A researcher who ships, not just studies

I'm a final-year Computer Science undergraduate at the University of Asia Pacific (CGPA 3.78/4.00), specializing in machine learning, deep learning, and computer vision. My work sits at the intersection of research rigor and production-grade engineering — I care about architectures that actually run under tight compute budgets, not just paper-friendly numbers.

My flagship research, MOI-Lite + E-SATF, is a compact intrusion-detection architecture benchmarked across three IoT security datasets (TON-IoT, UNSW-NB15, CIC-IDS2017), with a manuscript currently in preparation for IEEE submission. My thesis project fuses YOLOv11 with a Video Swin Transformer for real-time surveillance threat detection — combining object detection and spatio-temporal behavior modeling through a risk-scoring MLP.

Beyond research, I ship end-to-end: classical ML pipelines deployed on Hugging Face Spaces via Gradio, full-stack e-commerce migrations, and Java-based automation. I'm seeking an AI/ML Engineering role while applying for a Master's in Artificial Intelligence.
